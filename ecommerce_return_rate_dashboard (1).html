Phase 5 — Hypothesis Testing (3 Statistical Tests)
Statistical Test Results — All 3 Hypotheses Rejected at p < 0.05
T-Test · Chi-Square · One-Way ANOVA
✅ Reject H₀
T-Test (Delivery days): Returned orders avg 10.23 days vs 5.00 days for non-returned. T = 54.41, p = 0.000000. Delivery delay has a massive, statistically significant impact on returns.
✅ Reject H₀
Chi-Square (Delivery status): Delayed = 4,436 returned / 0 kept. On-Time = 3,305 returned / 3,258 kept. Chi² = 3126.55, p = 0.000000. Delivery status is strongly associated with return behavior.
✅ Reject H₀
ANOVA (Product cost by risk): Product cost differs significantly across Low/Medium/High return risk groups. F = 28.99, p = 0.000000. Cost is a meaningful predictor of return risk level.

Phase 7 — Business Recommendations
4 Priority Action Areas for Operations Team
Based on statistical findings across all 7 phases
🚚 Enforce 7-Day Delivery SLA

100% of delayed orders were returned. This is the single biggest lever. Every delivery beyond 7 days is a guaranteed return. Audit logistics partners immediately.

🏷 Cap Discounts at 35%

Orders with 0–10% and 51%+ discounts have highest returns. Counter-intuitively, very low discounts also spike returns — likely impulse buys. Cap at 35% for Furniture, Electronics, Sports.

📞 Alert When Care Calls ≥ 5

High care call frequency signals dissatisfaction before a return is filed. Proactive intervention when calls reach 5+ can convert potential returns into retained orders.

⭐ Outreach for Rating ≤ 2

Customers rating ≤ 2 return 100% of the time. Real-time rating monitoring + proactive outreach within 24 hours of a low rating can intercept returns before they happen.
